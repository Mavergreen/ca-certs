CA Certificates for Mavericks installs certificate data derived from Mozilla's NSS
(lib/ckfw/builtins/certdata.txt), which is distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0:
https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/

The exact source for each release is the NSS release named in NSS_TAG, fetched from
https://ftp.mozilla.org/pub/security/nss/releases/ and verified against Mozilla's SHA256SUMS.
